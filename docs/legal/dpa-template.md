# Data processing agreement (template)

> **DRAFT – requires lawyer review.** For a customer (e.g. a police department, a township, a fleet operator)
> that shares personal or confidential data with HariBatti.

**Parties:** [Customer] ("Data Fiduciary") and [HariBatti operator] ("Data Processor").

1. **Subject and duration.** Processing needed to provide HariBatti (signal audit, countdown, reports) during the
   pilot/subscription term in the order form.
2. **Data and people concerned.** Signal phase data (not personal); junction video (people and vehicles, blurred
   before storage); staff emails and roles; optional field-study GPS traces of consenting volunteers.
3. **Instructions.** The Processor processes only on the Fiduciary's documented instructions. HariBatti has no
   capability to control signals, and none will be added under this agreement.
4. **Location.** All storage and processing in India. No transfer abroad without written consent.
5. **Security.** HTTPS; encryption at rest; least-privilege access; audit logs; security testing (see SECURITY.md);
   staff confidentiality.
6. **Sub-processors.** Listed in Annex 1 (hosting provider in India). Changes notified 30 days in advance.
7. **Breach.** The Processor informs the Fiduciary without undue delay and within 24 hours of becoming aware, with
   the facts needed for the Fiduciary's notices under the DPDP Rules and to CERT-In (6-hour reporting).
8. **Data-principal requests.** The Processor helps answer access, correction and erasure requests (built-in export
   and erasure tools).
9. **Retention and return.** Retention as in the privacy policy (GPS and raw video 30 days). At the end of the term,
   data is returned in open formats (CSV/JSON/PDF) and then deleted within 30 days, with a written confirmation.
10. **Audit.** The Fiduciary may audit once a year, or after a breach, with reasonable notice.

**Annex 1 – sub-processors:** [hosting provider, India region] · **Annex 2 – security measures:** as in SECURITY.md.
