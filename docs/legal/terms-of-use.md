# Terms of use

> **DRAFT – requires lawyer review.**

1. **What HariBatti is.** A read-only information service: signal countdowns, speed advice and signal-timing audits.
   It never controls a traffic signal. Signal timings and countdowns may be wrong, late or missing.
2. **Road safety comes first.** Always obey the actual signal, road signs, police and the law. Speed advice never
   exceeds the speed limit minus 5 km/h and never tells you to "go". Do not use the app by hand while driving;
   the app locks taps above 5 km/h.
3. **No warranty.** The service is provided "as is". Numbers are labelled with their source (SIM, FIELD, SURVEY,
   CROWD, ITMS); simulated and assumed values are estimates, not measurements.
4. **Accounts (Signal Command).** Accounts are personal. Keep sign-in codes private. Do not try to access data of
   another organisation, test security outside the responsible-disclosure policy, or overload the service.
5. **Customer data.** Police and customer data (feeds, videos, timing plans) belong to the customer. We process it only
   to provide the service, as set out in the data processing agreement.
6. **Liability.** To the extent allowed by law, HariBatti is not liable for indirect loss, or for decisions made on the
   basis of its recommendations; officers decide and apply any timing change in their own systems.
7. **Law.** Indian law; courts at Jaipur, Rajasthan.
