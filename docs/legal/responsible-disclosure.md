# Responsible disclosure

> **DRAFT – requires lawyer review.** Not yet a binding policy.

We welcome reports from security researchers.

**In scope:** the website (hari-batti-jaipur.vercel.app), the Signal Command dashboard and API when hosted by us,
the HariBatti mobile app, and this repository.

**Out of scope:** physical traffic signals and any police or vendor system (HariBatti cannot control them — never
test them), denial-of-service, social engineering, spam, and reports from automated scanners without a proof of impact.

**How to report:** GitHub → Security → Report a vulnerability
(https://github.com/krish2105/Hari-Batti-Jaipur/security/advisories/new). Include steps to reproduce and the impact.

**Our promise:** reply within 5 working days, keep you informed, credit you (if you wish) once fixed, and not take
legal action for good-faith research that follows this policy: do not access more data than needed to show the issue,
do not modify or delete data, stop and report as soon as you find personal data, and give us reasonable time to fix
before any disclosure. Reports that involve Indian critical infrastructure may also be shared with CERT-In as the law requires.
